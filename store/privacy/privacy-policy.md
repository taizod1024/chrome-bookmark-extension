# Privacy policy

Chrome Bookmark Extension displays and manages bookmarks in the Chrome bookmarks bar.

## Data used

- **Bookmarks:** The extension reads bookmark titles, URLs, and folders to display and search them. It creates, updates, moves, or deletes bookmarks and folders when you request those actions.
- **Open tabs:** When you drop a URL without a title, the extension reads open-tab URLs and titles to find a matching title. If a match is found, the title is saved as the new bookmark name.
- **Preferences:** Layout and display settings are stored in the extension's local browser storage. Search text is reflected in the extension page's URL.
- **Favicons:** The extension uses Chrome's favicon cache to display icons for bookmarked sites.

## Sharing and retention

The extension processes this information locally. It does not transmit it to the developer or third parties, and does not sell it or use it for advertising or tracking.

Bookmarks remain in Chrome until you change or delete them. Settings remain in the extension's local storage until they are cleared or the extension is removed. Chrome's own bookmark synchronization, if enabled, is managed separately by Chrome.

Opening a bookmark navigates your browser to the selected website; that website's own privacy practices apply.
