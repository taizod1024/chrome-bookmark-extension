# Data usage certification

Before submission, review the Chrome Web Store's data-use questions and current Developer Program Policies. Disclose the extension's handling of bookmark data and open-tab URLs and titles, including local-only use; do not assume the data-type checkboxes can all be left unchecked.

- Bookmarks and tab information are read and processed locally.
- When a URL is dropped without a title, a matching tab title may be saved as the new bookmark name.
- Preferences (columns, rows, display flags) are stored locally via localStorage.
- The extension does not transmit or sell this data.
- Review the certification statements before confirming them.
- Publish the privacy policy at a publicly accessible URL and provide that URL in the developer dashboard.

References: [User Data FAQ](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq) and [Privacy practices](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/cws-dashboard-privacy).
